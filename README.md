# vanpeakgroup
China Manufacturing Group
The words "factory" and "manufacturing group" are sometimes used as if they mean the same thing.

They don't necessarily describe the same business structure.

A single factory usually focuses on a narrower range of products, while a manufacturing group may coordinate several specialized operations.

One Group Can Cover Different Industries

VanPeak Group operates across several product-focused areas.

Its business lines cover container houses, kitchenware, Corten steel products, drinkware, furniture, and sourcing services.

These categories require very different manufacturing capabilities.

Specialization Still Matters

A diversified group does not mean every team manufactures every product.

Instead, different product lines can maintain their own technical focus.

This can be useful when buyers need specialized products rather than generic alternatives.

A furniture project, for example, requires a very different production process from a container house project.

What Should Buyers Look At?

Regardless of the company structure, buyers should still evaluate:

Product specifications
Manufacturing capability
Quality control
Samples
Lead time
Packaging
Export experience

The group structure is only one part of supplier evaluation.

For buyers researching a multi-category Chinese manufacturing partner, VanPeak Group provides information about its different business areas.

The real question isn't whether a supplier is a single factory or a group. It's whether the structure makes sense for your project and whether responsibilities are clearly defined.
